Honda documents its broad Brazilian adoption, engine, gearbox and step-through practicality in the [2025 launch release](https://saladeimprensa.honda.com.br/releases/honda-biz-2025-motor-inedito-sistema-de-frenagem-aprimorado-evolucao-no-design-e-em). The fast bike represents the **Suzuki Hayabusa GSX1300R, model year 2022**, with a 1340 cc liquid-cooled DOHC inline-four and six-speed transmission, documented in [Suzuki's model page](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/motorcycle/smgs/products/2022hayabusa/) and [manufacturer technical sheet](https://suzuki-adplanner-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/features/_2022/sportbike/fbs-gsx1300rrqm2e.pdf). The game uses 299 km/h, 2.84 s 0–100, a 24,000-coin price, idle/redline synthesis at 1050/11000 RPM and tuned gear-speed thresholds. These are game values; no manufacturer-certified acceleration time, rev limiter or exact recording is claimed. Its six-gear four-cylinder harmonic profile differs from Raio 1000.

Measured 0–100 and 70/90/95% times use the real Race at 20 ms steps, traffic disabled, unlimited fuel and centered steering: [acceleration table](acceleration-v6.md). Both bikes inherit the existing narrow collision reach and rain-grip tradeoff. Existing saved IDs remain unchanged. The ladder Uno costs 3,200 coins for new purchases, above Maverick's 1,600; its 400 km/h tuning and previously owned status remain intact.
