# Vehicle and presentation tuning v3

These are arcade tuning values, not measurements of real vehicles.

- Vehicle widths and dry-road braking rates live in `src/game/vehicle-tuning.ts`. The Kombi is 1.44 times the Fusca's visual width, with a taller authored silhouette. SUV, muscle cars, compacts and motorcycles have individual sizes. Sprite scaling remains uniform; collision/pickup reach follows the player's width. Garage and menu previews now use one scale for the whole fleet instead of enlarging each model to fill its thumbnail independently.
- Acceleration is `car.accel * 0.25 * (1 - (speed / car.max)^2)`, with the existing off-road traction factor. The Fusca takes roughly 18 seconds of simulation to reach 100 km/h and more than 40 seconds to reach 95% of its maximum; the GT reaches 100 in less than 8 seconds. Acceleration tapers near the maximum instead of abruptly hitting a limiter.
- Braking is individual per vehicle: Kombi 29, Fusca 34, Brasilia 36, Chevette 35, Uno 38, SUV 39, Opala 41, CG 125 42, Palio 44, Maverick 45, Gol 46, urban bike 48, superbike 64 and GT 70 km/h per second. Rain reduces braking grip, more strongly for motorcycles.
- Music bus gain is 0.07 in the menu and 0.0455 while racing, down from 0.32. Effects gain is 0.20. The engine gets more gain and a wider filter when accelerating. Brake friction uses a broad filter with a quiet, model-specific tonal component; the GT has no tonal squeal and a much lower friction centre frequency.
- Fog extends above the horizon into the skyline and sky, with its strongest density around the distant city/horizon. Its tint darkens at night. Nearby road and vehicles remain progressively clearer.
- A continuation costs **1,000 coins**, at most once per run. `Race.resume(wallet)` checks status, previous continuation and balance before debiting the wallet. Rejected attempts do not charge. The application persists the debit immediately; insufficient-balance buttons are disabled and show the current balance. Run rewards retain their settlement counter, preventing duplicate payouts after continuing.
- Main-menu **Buy coins** opens a preview of 1,000 / 3,000 / 7,500 coin packs at BRL 9.90 / 24.90 / 49.90. Purchases are explicitly unavailable and buttons are disabled until a payment provider and verified server-side credit delivery are integrated. No payment provider has been selected; no real-money checkout is implemented.
