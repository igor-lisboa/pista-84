# Classic vehicle artwork and driving presentation v2

The six newer vehicles now use [vehicle-classics-v2.png](../web/assets/vehicle-classics-v2.png), generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the original vehicle atlas as a style reference. The final project asset has genuine alpha transparency and 18 complete sprites. No API/CLI generation was used.

Rows: Kombi, Uno with ladder, Chevette, CG 125, Opala SS, Maverick GT. Columns: straight rear, gentle right turn, hard right turn. Left turns mirror the authored right frames. Each pose is cropped by alpha bounds, recolored for paint selections, and uniformly scaled by the same renderer as the original eight vehicles. The procedural artwork remains an asset-loading fallback.

The source is 1024 × 1536. Authored row cuts are `0, 284, 548, 770, 1036, 1280, 1536` for the first two columns; the third column uses `772` instead of `770` between Chevette and CG 125. Column cuts are `0, 320, 662, 1024`. All 18 cells have transparent borders. Cropping at equal row intervals would cut through tyres and the ladder.

The gameplay camera is always 960 × 720 (4:3), fitted with `object-fit: contain`. Portrait, landscape and square retain the complete frame with space around it as necessary. Road width, car-to-lane proportions, horizon, visible road distance and reaction time no longer depend on viewport shape or the previous 600px resolution breakpoint. HUD and controls remain responsive around that frame.

The visible road spans 104 m rather than 240 m, giving 150 km/h approximately the old 350 km/h approach rate. Metres, km/h, checkpoints, score and fuel retain their physical units. Cornering load now uses `clamp((speed / 120)^2, 0, 6)`, requiring more correction at highway speed and braking on extreme-speed bends. Visual intensity uses `clamp((speed - 35) / 115, 0, 2.75)`; streak count/length, road rumble and wind noise continue rising to 350 km/h. Vignette and streak opacity stay bounded to keep traffic readable.

Run `node scripts/review-driving.ts` with the development server running to check all atlas cell borders and save a pose contact sheet plus 120/150/350 km/h scenes at all three screen shapes in `docs/screenshots/driving-v2/`.

```text

Input image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match its richly shaded crisp pixel illustration, dark outline, highlights, rear chase camera elevated perspective. Create exactly THREE COLUMNS and SIX ROWS in equal sized cells. 18 COMPLETE isolated vehicles with generous transparent gutters in every cell, no text, labels, grid, scenery, floor, shadows, front views or watermark. Each ROW is ONE vehicle with consistent paint/details/proportions across 3 poses: column 1 STRAIGHT REAR, column 2 gentle RIGHT TURN 12 degrees yaw, column 3 hard RIGHT TURN 25 degrees yaw, showing real left side panels and windows extending away toward upper right; all driving AWAY from viewer. Preserve entire wheels mirrors and body within each cell. Row 1: 1978 Volkswagen Kombi Brazilian bay-window van, turquoise lower body cream white upper body/roof, broad rear window, engine hatch cooling slots, vertical small rear lamps, chrome bumper. Row 2: white Fiat Uno square hatchback with long aluminum LADDER strapped lengthwise on roof, vertical corner rear lights and black bumper. Row 3: burnt orange 1982 Chevrolet Chevette two door sedan, classic angular roof, rear windshield and short trunk, rectangular rear lamps chrome bumper. Row 4: red Honda CG 125 small commuter motorcycle, helmeted rider black jacket blue jeans, red delivery box, thin rear tire and chrome exhaust; real right leaning rider and bike in turn poses. Row 5: blue Chevrolet Opala SS classic Brazilian coupe, black racing stripes, long trunk, wide rear rectangular tail lamps chrome bumper. Row 6: orange Ford Maverick GT 1970s muscle coupe, black rear panel with round red taillights, fastback roof, wide rear tires, twin exhaust. Match original reference quality: dimensional metal bodywork, glass reflections, layered pixel shading, finely drawn mechanical details, believable compact rear silhouettes. Exactly 3 columns x 6 rows. Keep each car centered within cell with same ground baseline and physical size across all three poses. Output large portrait atlas.
```
